Probability
TIFR-CAM, August-December, 2026.

Classes will be held from 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM on Mondays and 2:00 PM to 3:30 PM on Wednesdays and 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM on Fridays. One day a week will be left for a short quiz and tutorial (might change from week to week) and for clearing any doubts that you may have. Please send me your doubts before hand.
Assessment: Assessment will be based on assignments, quizes, the midterm and the final term. There is no provision for a re-exam.
Grading Scheme: 40 (Final term) + 25 (midterm) + 15 (assignments) + 20 (quizes); solutions from bonus problems can be used to improve the net midterm score. In any form of assessment (exam, assignment, quiz), inability to explain the solution will imply 0 on that form of assessment. For example, if you wrote a solution in your assignment which you can't explain, you will not just lose points for that assignment but for all assignments. This also sums up our policy with regard to the use of LLMs and AI in the course. I think the internet and LLMs are great places to learn material. However I would encourage you to talk to your friends, Debjit or me before you plug this into an LLM or search on the google. Getting stuck is a great way to learn.
